---
id: open-access-overuse
claim: A resource open to all with no rules governing its use tends to be overused, because each user gains the full benefit of extra use while the cost is shared.
evidence:
  level: established
  kinds: [theory, observation]
  why: Consistent with economic theory and with many observed cases of open-access depletion, such as some fisheries.
agreement:
  status: leaning
  why: Accepted for genuinely open-access resources. As a claim about commons in general it is contested (see the objection).
  wouldChange: Evidence of open-access resources without rules that are not overused under pressure.
objection: Hardin conflated open access with commons. Ostrom and others showed that many commons have rules made by their users and are not overused, so "tragedy" is not the fate of commons as such.
unknowns: How quickly informal norms emerge to govern open resources, and when they do not.
