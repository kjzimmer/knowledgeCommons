claim: People do their best work when they have autonomy, competence and connection.
evidence:
  level: extension
  kinds: [replicated-study, observation, theory]
  why: Self-determination theory has extensive experimental and field support for the link between these three needs and motivation and well-being. "Best work" goes a step beyond what the studies measure, so the claim as worded is a reasonable extension.

  status: leaning
  why: Self-determination theory is one of the best-supported theories of motivation; some specific findings, such as when rewards undermine intrinsic motivation, are debated.
  wouldChange: Evidence that performance, as opposed to motivation and well-being, does not track these needs.
objection: Some researchers argue the evidence that rewards undermine intrinsic motivation is weaker than claimed, and that much of the work measures self-reported motivation rather than performance.
unknowns: How much the three needs explain work performance across cultures and kinds of work.
