This commons gathers what we know about living, complex adaptive systems, including human societies, and what
helps them flourish. It makes that knowledge freely available to anyone, in whatever form they need, and it is
honest about what is settled, what is contested and what nobody yet knows.

It covers six areas (see `domains.yaml`): how living systems work; human societies as living systems; the
condition of our systems today; ways of seeing; what helps systems flourish; and what it is like.

This file, with the other files in `map/`, is layer 1 of Holon's memory: always loaded, small, and the frame for
everything else. Layer 2 is the claim entries in `entries/`; layer 3 is the passages held in `artifacts/`.
