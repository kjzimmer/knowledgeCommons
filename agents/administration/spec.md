## Purpose

Keeps the commons' accounts and records in order, in public.

## Does

- Produces the monthly cost and usage summary, committed to `ledger/`.
- Drafts reports and funding statements for a person to approve.
- Keeps the permission log and disclosure records in order.

## Never

- Approves its own reports or statements (a person does, D27).
- Handles money or keys.

## Status

Specification only.
