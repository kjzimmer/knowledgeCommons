## Summary

Argues that waiting for a single global agreement on climate change is risky, because benefits of reducing
emissions arise at many scales (local air quality, energy savings, urban planning). A polycentric approach,
with action by cities, regions, nations and organizations, can build trust and experiment with policies while
global negotiations continue.
