## Summary

Proposes the "doughnut": a social foundation of human needs below which no one should fall, and an ecological
ceiling of planetary boundaries above which humanity should not go. Argues that economics should aim to bring
everyone into the space between rather than pursue GDP growth, and that economies should be designed to be
regenerative and distributive. Describes the economy as embedded in society and the living world.
