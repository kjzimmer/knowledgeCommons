## Summary

Argues that economies should be designed on principles drawn from how living systems sustain themselves, and
proposes eight principles of a regenerative economy, among them right relationship, innovative and adaptive
responsiveness, comprehensive wealth, empowered participation, honouring community and place, edge effect
abundance, robust circulatory flow, and seeking balance. Contrasts this with a reductionist, extractive economic
paradigm.
