## Summary

Argues that networked information technology makes "commons-based peer production" possible: large numbers of
people cooperating to produce information goods, such as free software and Wikipedia, without relying on either
market prices or managerial command. Benkler argues this mode of production can expand individual autonomy,
democratic participation and development.
