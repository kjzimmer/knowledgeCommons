## Summary

Presents the Cynefin framework for leaders: simple (later "clear"), complicated, complex and chaotic contexts,
plus disorder. In complicated contexts cause and effect can be found by analysis or expertise; in complex contexts
they are only clear in hindsight, so leaders should probe, sense and respond, letting patterns emerge, rather than
impose a plan. Mistaking a complex situation for a complicated one is a common failure.
