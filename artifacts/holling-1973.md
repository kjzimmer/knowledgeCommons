## Summary

The founding paper of resilience thinking in ecology. Distinguishes stability (the ability of a system to return
to equilibrium after a disturbance) from resilience (the persistence of relationships within a system, its ability
to absorb change and still persist). Argues that a system can be highly resilient yet fluctuate greatly, and that
managing for stability and a maximum steady yield can erode resilience, leaving systems vulnerable to sudden
collapse.
