## Summary

Proposes nine "planetary boundaries" (among them climate change, biodiversity loss, the nitrogen and phosphorus
cycles, ocean acidification, land use and freshwater use) that define a safe operating space for humanity, and
estimates that three (climate change, rate of biodiversity loss, and the nitrogen cycle) had already been crossed.
Later updates revised the framework.
