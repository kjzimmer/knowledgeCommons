## Summary

An expert elicitation: researchers from several disciplines judged how much climate has affected organized armed
conflict. They agreed climate has affected conflict risk, but ranked it low among drivers compared with factors such
as low socioeconomic development and low state capacity, while judging that its influence may grow with warming.
The method is structured expert judgement, not a new empirical study.
