## Summary

Opens a BMJ series on complexity science in health care. Argues that patients, clinical teams and health systems
behave as complex adaptive systems, so that linear planning and detailed specification often fail. Suggests
approaches such as working with a few simple rules ("minimum specifications"), using attractors, and learning by
experiment.
