## Summary

Argues that the economic problem is not how to allocate given resources but how to use knowledge that is never
given to anyone in total: knowledge of particular circumstances of time and place, dispersed among many people.
The price system works as a mechanism for communicating that information, letting people adjust to changes they
know nothing about directly. Central planning cannot gather this knowledge.
