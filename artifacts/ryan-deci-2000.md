## Summary

Summarizes self-determination theory: people have three basic psychological needs, for autonomy, competence and
relatedness, and social conditions that support them foster intrinsic motivation, well-internalized extrinsic
motivation, and well-being, while conditions that thwart them undermine both. Reviews experimental and field
research, including findings that some kinds of external rewards and controls can reduce intrinsic motivation.
