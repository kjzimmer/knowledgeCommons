## Summary

Argues that the dominant biomedical model, which reduces illness to measurable biological variables, cannot account
for how people actually fall ill, experience illness and recover. Proposes a biopsychosocial model that takes
psychological and social factors into account alongside biological ones, drawing on general systems theory.
