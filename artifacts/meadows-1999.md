## Summary

Meadows lists twelve places to intervene in a system, from the least effective (constants and parameters such as
subsidies, taxes and standards) to the most effective (the mindset or paradigm out of which the system arises, and
the power to transcend paradigms). In between come buffers, stock-and-flow structure, delays, balancing and
reinforcing feedback loops, information flows, rules, the power to self-organize, and goals. She argues that
people often find a system's leverage points intuitively but push them in the wrong direction, and she presents
the list as tentative.

## Quotations

- "It is tentative and its order is slithery." (introduction to the list)
- "Be sure you change it in the right direction!" (on finding a leverage point)
