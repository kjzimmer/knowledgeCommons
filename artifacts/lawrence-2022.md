## Summary

Proposes a working definition: a global polycrisis occurs when crises in multiple global systems become causally
entangled in ways that significantly degrade humanity's prospects. The paper distinguishes a polycrisis from a
systemic risk, treating polycrisis as the realized, entangled condition. *From the model's knowledge, not checked
against the PDF:* it describes how crises in one system can be transmitted to or amplified by others, and calls for
research on the causal links between global systems.
