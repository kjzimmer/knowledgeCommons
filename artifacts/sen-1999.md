## Summary

Argues that development should be judged by the expansion of the real freedoms people enjoy, their
"capabilities" to live lives they have reason to value, rather than by income or growth alone. Income matters as a
means; political freedoms, economic facilities, social opportunities, transparency and protective security are
both ends and means.
