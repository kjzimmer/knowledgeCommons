## Summary

Describes climate governance as a "regime complex": loosely coupled institutions rather than one integrated
regime. The authors argue a comprehensive regime is unlikely, and that a regime complex offers flexibility and
adaptability. They set out criteria for judging such a complex and judge the climate case to fall short on several.
